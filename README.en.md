ViBilet

A digital pet that grows, evolves, and passes something on through generations of life.

ViBilet is a digital life simulation and pet-raising mobile application currently being developed with Flutter and Dart.

Players care for small digital lifeforms called "LIFE" that are born inside their smartphones, nurturing them through feeding, interaction, and everyday care.

Each LIFE grows in real time and eventually reaches the end of its lifespan.

The game aims to create a world where growth is shaped by the time spent together and the care each LIFE receives, while some of its traits and memories can be passed on to the next generation.

Concept

ViBilet explores the idea of living alongside digital lifeforms in a virtual environment.

Each LIFE has its own condition and daily rhythm, and time continues to pass even when the player is not actively using the application.

Rather than constantly controlling their LIFE, players observe its behavior and provide care when needed.

Every LIFE also has a finite lifespan.

Through birth, growth, farewell, and the passing of traits to future generations, ViBilet aims to explore the relationship between players and digital lifeforms.

Features

Living with LIFE

The application manages the lifecycle of each LIFE, from birth and naming to daily care and the end of its lifespan.

- Birth and naming
- State changes based on real-world time
- Feeding, interaction, and sleeping
- Hunger and bonding systems
- Status information through LIFE DATA
- Animations reflecting different actions and conditions

Growth & Evolution

Every LIFE begins as "PIX", a shared initial form.

As it grows, an "EVOLUTION PATCH" can be applied to guide its development along different evolutionary paths.

Evolution is treated as the continued growth of the same individual, rather than replacing it with a different character.

Note: Growth and evolution features, along with the associated character assets, are still under development.

JUNK DATA

As LIFE inhabits its digital environment, unnecessary data known as "JUNK DATA" gradually accumulates.

Depending on the amount accumulated, objects and visual noise appear in the environment, progressively distorting the display.

This system visually represents the condition of the digital environment through graphical effects.

MEMORY DATA

"MEMORY DATA" is a system designed to allow certain traits and characteristics of a LIFE that has reached the end of its natural lifespan to be passed on to the next generation.

Each new LIFE is born as an independent individual, but may retain traces of its predecessor.

Note: The underlying inheritance logic has been implemented, while its in-game presentation and related assets remain under development.

Development

ViBilet is an independent, solo-developed project.

The game systems, user interface, characters, and animations are being developed incrementally.

Current development focuses on implementing and refining the core lifecycle and pet-care features, alongside character assets and visual effects.
